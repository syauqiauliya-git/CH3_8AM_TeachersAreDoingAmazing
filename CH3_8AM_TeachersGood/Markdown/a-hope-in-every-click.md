# A Hope in Every Click of a Waning Finger

## The Inspiring Story of Mrs. Lorena S. Mendoza: A Cancer and Illiteracy Fighter

## Introduction

When schools across the Philippines were forced to adapt to new ways of teaching during the COVID-19 pandemic, many educators faced unfamiliar challenges. Among them was Mrs. Lorena S. Mendoza, a veteran teacher whose determination to continue educating her students became an inspiring example of resilience, lifelong learning, and dedication to service. Despite battling breast cancer and having limited experience with technology, she refused to let obstacles stand in the way of her students' education. (Depeddasma)

---

## A Life Dedicated to Teaching

Mrs. Mendoza had been teaching Filipino at Langkaan Elementary School for 25 years. Throughout her career, she taught multiple generations of students, including the children of some of her former pupils.

Although she lived far from the school, she chose to remain there because of her strong connection to the community and her colleagues. Every day, she travelled long distances to continue serving the students she cared about deeply. Her commitment to teaching never wavered, earning the admiration and respect of those around her. (Depeddasma)

---

## A Battle Against Cancer

In March 2019, Mrs. Mendoza was diagnosed with breast cancer. She underwent surgery the following month and took a seven-month leave from work to recover.

Her family hoped she would focus entirely on her health, but she missed the classroom immensely. According to her, the silence of being away from her students was harder to endure than many of the medical procedures she experienced. Teaching had become an inseparable part of her identity. After months of recovery, she returned to work in January 2020. (Depeddasma)

---

## New Challenges in a New Normal

Shortly after returning, schools faced a series of disruptions, including the Taal Volcano eruption and later the COVID-19 pandemic.

As education shifted to distance learning, teachers had to choose between modular and online instruction. Initially, Mrs. Mendoza preferred modular learning because she believed technology would be difficult for her to master. She openly admitted that information and communication technology had always been her weakest area.

However, because she was still undergoing chemotherapy, regularly handling printed learning materials posed health risks. To protect herself while continuing to teach, she made the courageous decision to join the online learning programme. (Depeddasma)

---

## Learning Without Fear

Mrs. Mendoza knew the transition would not be easy. Nevertheless, she embraced the challenge with determination.

Using only her laptop and mobile phone, she dedicated time every day to learning new digital skills. She watched online tutorials, attended training sessions, and practised independently. Whenever she encountered difficulties, she sought help from her children and younger colleagues.

Little by little, she learned how to use digital tools, create online presentations, and build virtual classrooms. What once seemed impossible gradually became achievable through persistence and patience. (Depeddasma)

---

## Inspiring Others Through Growth

Despite considering herself a beginner, Mrs. Mendoza never stopped learning. She proudly shared her work with fellow teachers and remained eager to improve.

Her willingness to adapt inspired many of her younger colleagues. They admired not only her dedication to mastering technology but also her determination to continue teaching while undergoing cancer treatment.

She demonstrated that learning does not end with age or experience. Instead, growth is possible whenever people remain open to change and committed to their goals. (Depeddasma)

---

## A Teacher's Unbreakable Spirit

While many people struggled with the sudden changes brought about by the pandemic, Mrs. Mendoza chose to focus on possibilities rather than limitations.

She fought two battles simultaneously: a personal battle against cancer and a professional challenge of adapting to a new educational environment. Through both, she remained focused on one mission—ensuring that her students continued to learn. Her story became a symbol of hope and perseverance during uncertain times. (Depeddasma)

---

## Lessons from Mrs. Mendoza's Story

Mrs. Mendoza's journey teaches valuable lessons about resilience, adaptability, and lifelong learning.

### Key Takeaways

- Dedication to a purpose can provide strength during difficult times.
- Learning new skills is possible at any age.
- Challenges can become opportunities for growth.
- Technology can empower educators to continue serving their students.
- Perseverance and optimism inspire others to do the same.

---

## Conclusion

Mrs. Lorena S. Mendoza's story is a powerful reminder that true educators never stop learning. Even while fighting cancer and facing the uncertainties of a global pandemic, she embraced change and continued to put her students first.

Her journey demonstrates that courage is not the absence of fear or difficulty—it is the willingness to move forward despite them. Through every lesson she taught and every new skill she learned, Mrs. Mendoza proved that hope can be found in every challenge and that education remains a force capable of overcoming even the greatest obstacles. (Depeddasma)
