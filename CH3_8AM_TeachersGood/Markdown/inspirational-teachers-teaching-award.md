# Inspirational Teachers: Teaching Award Winners Share Who Made an Impact on Them

## Introduction

In 2020, several recipients of the Lifetime Achievement Silver Teaching Award reflected on the teachers who inspired them and shared what they love most about teaching. Their stories highlight the lasting influence that great educators can have on young people's lives. (Teaching Blog)

---

## David Horn: Building Confidence

David remembers his English teacher, Mr Hudgell, who stood out not only because of his passion for literature but because he genuinely cared about his students.

As a teenager, David lacked confidence. One day, Mr Hudgell asked him to read and review Lord of the Flies, making him feel trusted and valued. The experience boosted David's self-esteem and left a lasting impression that he still remembers decades later. (Teaching Blog)

### What David Loves About Teaching

David enjoys seeing students grow in confidence and achieve things they once thought impossible. He also loves the complexity of teaching, describing it as a craft that requires constant learning, creativity, and collaboration. (Teaching Blog)

---

## Andrew Szydlo: Inspiring Curiosity

Andrew credits his science teacher, Mr Burrow, as a major influence. Although strict, Mr Burrow captivated students with exciting chemistry experiments and a strong sense of humour, making science both engaging and memorable. (Teaching Blog)

### What Andrew Loves About Teaching

Andrew's passion for chemistry is matched by his enjoyment of answering students' questions. He finds satisfaction in helping young people learn and believes that children are naturally enthusiastic when taught in the right way. (Teaching Blog)

---

## Anthony Bennett: Encouraging Creativity

Anthony was fortunate to learn from several inspiring teachers. He particularly remembers teachers who encouraged creativity, independent thinking, and intellectual curiosity.

He also recognises the influence of mentors, family members, and community leaders who guided and supported young people outside the classroom. (Teaching Blog)

### What Anthony Loves About Teaching

Anthony values the opportunity to work with talented students and colleagues. He believes teaching keeps people connected to the world around them and provides a unique opportunity to inspire creativity and personal growth. (Teaching Blog)

---

## Paul Phillips: Passion and High Expectations

Paul recalls his English teacher, Miss Duffield, whose passion for her subject and commitment to her students made a lasting impact on him. Her high standards and dedication inspired him long after he left school. (Teaching Blog)

### What Paul Loves About Teaching

Paul enjoys creating meaningful change in both individuals and society. As a senior leader, he has focused on helping learners realise their potential and believes education can transform lives, communities, and opportunities. (Teaching Blog)

---

## Key Themes

Across all four stories, several common themes emerge:

- Great teachers show genuine care for their students.
- Confidence can be transformed by a single meaningful act.
- Passion for a subject inspires lifelong learning.
- Teaching is about helping people realise their potential.
- Educators continue learning and growing throughout their careers.

These award-winning educators demonstrate that the impact of a teacher can last a lifetime, influencing not only academic achievement but also confidence, ambition, and personal development. (Teaching Blog)
