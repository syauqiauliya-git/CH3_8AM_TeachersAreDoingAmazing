# A Rare Dedication to Education

## Introduction

In Barangay Tabangao Ambulong, Batangas, one teacher's story demonstrates the dedication, sacrifices, and challenges that many educators face every day. Cristina A. Farrell, affectionately known as Ma'am Teng, devoted her life to helping students learn and grow, often placing their needs alongside those of her own family. After more than 14 years in public education, her journey became a powerful reminder of both the rewards and costs of teaching. (Pilipinas Shell Foundation, Inc. -)

---

## A Passion for Teaching

Ma'am Teng discovered her passion for teaching early in her career. At Tabangao Elementary School, she became known for her commitment, efficiency, and willingness to take on additional responsibilities.

Beyond teaching Grade 5 students, she served as a caretaker of school facilities, managed inventories, coordinated school improvement activities, and acted as a second parent to many children. Her dedication stemmed from a genuine love for her profession. (Pilipinas Shell Foundation, Inc. -)

---

## The Challenges Behind the Classroom

Like many public school teachers, Ma'am Teng faced numerous difficulties. She managed large classes, long working hours, limited resources, and financial pressures despite her hard work.

At home, her husband and five children helped support her demanding career. They assisted with preparing teaching materials, creating classroom decorations, and helping with household responsibilities. Teaching became a family effort. (Pilipinas Shell Foundation, Inc. -)

---

## A Difficult Sacrifice

After years of service, Ma'am Teng was transferred to another school located farther from home. While the move represented professional growth, it also required a significant personal sacrifice.

Her daily commute meant leaving before her children woke up and returning after they had already gone to sleep. Slowly, she realised that although she was helping hundreds of students, she was missing important moments in her own children's lives. (Pilipinas Shell Foundation, Inc. -)

---

## The Moment of Realisation

One day, while sorting through classroom materials, Ma'am Teng found an identification photo and asked a student to return it to what she believed was another child.

The student gently corrected her.

The photograph belonged to Ma'am Teng's own daughter.

The incident shocked her. She realised she had never seen her daughter in her school uniform on picture day because work had kept her so occupied. What seemed like a simple mistake revealed how much of her family life she had unintentionally missed. (Pilipinas Shell Foundation, Inc. -)

---

## Reconnecting with Family

Determined to rebuild those connections, Ma'am Teng sought an opportunity to return to a school closer to home. Eventually, she was able to work again in Tabangao.

The move allowed her to spend more time with her family and reconnect with the moments she had been missing. At the same time, it renewed her love for teaching—not as an obligation, but as a passion she could pursue while maintaining a healthier balance between work and home life. (Pilipinas Shell Foundation, Inc. -)

---

## Lessons from Ma'am Teng's Story

Ma'am Teng's experience highlights the often unseen sacrifices teachers make in service of their students. Her story reminds us that educators play an essential role in society, but they also need support, resources, fair compensation, and opportunities to maintain a balance between their professional and personal lives. (Pilipinas Shell Foundation, Inc. -)

### Key Takeaways

- Passion can inspire extraordinary dedication.
- Teaching often involves sacrifices beyond the classroom.
- Family support can help educators overcome challenges.
- Success should not come at the expense of personal relationships.
- Teachers deserve greater recognition, resources, and support.

---

## Conclusion

Ma'am Teng's journey is both inspiring and cautionary. Her commitment transformed the lives of countless students, but it also taught her the importance of being present for the people closest to her. Through her story, we gain a deeper appreciation for educators and the balancing act they perform every day in shaping the future while caring for their own families. (Pilipinas Shell Foundation, Inc. -)
